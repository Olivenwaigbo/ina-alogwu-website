ADVISORY SERVICES

Advice from someone who has built and run it.

Ina Alogwu advises banks, fintechs, telcos and investors on three things: payments, AI project execution and digital business transformation. Each area below shows where the experience comes from and how he can help.

01 / PAYMENTS ADVISORY

Payments strategy from someone who has run the rails.

Independent advice on payments strategy, products, partnerships, and fraud and trust, for banks, fintechs, telcos and investors.

Where the experience comes from

  • Interswitch Group, 2008–2021. Progressive leadership across payments, product strategy, retail services, Verve Digital, security and infrastructure.
  • Fraud management and payment processing at one of Africa's leading payments companies.
  • Non-Executive Director, 9 Payment Service Bank (since September 2024).
  • T2 Mobile. Leading the move into digital lifestyle and financial services: embedded payments, wallets and partner marketplaces.

How Ina can help

  • Payments strategy review. Where to play, and why products are not scaling.
  • Fraud and trust review. Control gaps across the payment journey and a prioritised action plan.
  • Product and business-model design. From idea to a model that works, with partners mapped.
  • Retained executive adviser. A sounding board for CEOs and boards.
