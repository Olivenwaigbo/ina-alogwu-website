01 / PROFILE

A practitioner who understands the ecosystem from the inside.

Ina Alogwu is a transformational leader in digital innovation, fintech and venture building, with experience designing and scaling Pan-African digital payment and financial services ecosystems across telecommunications, banking and digital commerce.

As Chief Digital & Innovation Officer at T2 Mobile, he leads the company's evolution into a digital lifestyle and financial services partner, driving embedded payments, wallets, partner marketplaces and platform-based services.

PAYMENTS EXPERIENCE

Two decades inside Africa's payments rails.

Ina has spent more than twenty years building and running digital payment and financial services businesses — from fraud management, payment processing and retail payment channels at Interswitch, to group digital transformation at ARM and digital services at T2 Mobile.
